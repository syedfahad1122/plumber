Common questions

How fast can mold start after a leak?

The EPA's mold guidance is built around responding to water damage within 24 to 48 hours, before growth starts. Florida's heat and humidity make same-day drying worth it. EPA mold remediation guidance

Is water from a sewage backup or flood dangerous?

Yes. Sewage and floodwater are treated as contaminated (Category 3) water. Keep children and pets away, wear boots and gloves, and leave cleanup of soaked carpet and drywall to professionals.

How long does drying take?

It depends on how much got wet and what it is made of. Professionals measure moisture each day and stop when readings are back to normal. Use our calculator for a rough planning range. Cost and drying calculator

Will insurance pay for it?

Sudden leaks like a burst pipe are often covered by homeowners insurance, while flood and storm surge need a separate flood policy, and slow leaks over time are often excluded. Many restoration companies work with insurers, so ask when you call, and check your policy. Insurance claim guide
