What to Do in the First 60 Minutes of a Burst Pipe

The first hour after a water pipe bursts determines whether building materials can be dried in place or require expensive wall demolition. Follow these steps immediately while our mobile unit is en route:

1
Shut Off Main Water Supply: Turn off your main domestic water valve (usually near your water meter or main shutoff valve outside/utility closet).
2
Relieve Faucet Line Pressure: Open the lowest and highest cold water faucets in your building to drain excess standing water out of the lines.
3
Turn Off Electrical Breakers: If water is leaking through ceiling fixtures or electrical outlets, shut off power to affected zones at your circuit breaker box.
4
Call 24/7 Mobile Dispatch: Call our hotline at (800) 948-4321 for immediate 35-minute mobile arrival across Florida.
