Burst pipe and plumbing leaks in Florida

Emergency plumbing repair and cleanup after burst or leaking pipes. Free 24/7 line to reach a local company.

Call (800) 948-4321

A burst supply line can release hundreds of gallons in an hour. In Florida, common causes are aging polybutylene or galvanized pipes, high water pressure, corroded fittings and failed supply hoses. You need two things fast: a plumber to stop and repair the leak, and a restoration company to dry the home.

What the work usually involves

  1. Shut off the main water valve right away.
  2. A licensed plumber finds and repairs the failed pipe or fitting.
  3. Extract water and open walls or cabinets where water traveled.
  4. Dry the wall cavities, subfloor and cabinets.
  5. Repair drywall, flooring and cabinets once readings are dry.

Signs you need help

  • Sudden drop in water pressure
  • Water bill much higher than normal
  • Sound of running water with taps off
  • Bulging or wet ceiling or wall

Burst pipe and plumbing leaks questions

Where is my main water shut-off?

In many Florida homes it's outside, near the meter at the street or where the line enters the house, often in a small box in the ground or on an outside wall.

Should I call a plumber or a restoration company first?

Stop the water first, then call both. A plumber fixes the pipe; a restoration company removes water and dries the home. Our line can connect you with a local company that can help.

Is it free to call?

Yes. Calling is free for homeowners. We're a referral service: the local company you're connected with pays us for the introduction, and you agree on the work and price directly with them.

Who will come to my home?

An independent, local water damage restoration or plumbing company that serves your area. Ask them about their Florida licensing, insurance and certifications (such as IICRC) when you talk.

Do you do the restoration work yourselves?

No. We don't employ crews or own equipment. We connect you with independent local companies.
